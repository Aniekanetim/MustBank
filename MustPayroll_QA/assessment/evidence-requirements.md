# What evidence does the Quest require?

Sources reviewed: the supplied original role-page MHTML and Quest Expectation - Full Transcript.txt. The project is adapted to payroll.

## Required by the original role page

The defect-report instruction includes “root cause, evidence and a proposed fix”. The final submission instruction says: “Include accessible links to your work and verification evidence in a results/handoff appendix in directive.md.”

The page also requires demonstrating the regression failing against faulty behavior and passing after correction, demonstrating the final test run, and linking eight or more cases and results. Written claims alone cannot establish those outcomes.

## What the transcript adds

Lines 622–624 require the results to belong to the assessed version and link a claimed pass to the executed check through a log. Lines 668–676 repeat the verification-evidence appendix and evidence in the defect report. Lines 836–850 explain reviewer access.

## Required evidence versus optional format

The supplied sources do not prescribe screenshots, a screenshot count, Playwright videos or trace archives as separate deliverables. Our reports, console logs, observed state, build identities and source fingerprints provide the verification evidence. Screenshots and browser recordings are optional supporting formats. The CSV column named Comment / Screenshot follows the candidate's reference template; that label does not create an assessment requirement.

The candidate's Loom of no more than five minutes is explicitly required. Optional automation recordings do not replace it.

## Where this project keeps the work

The two [execution folders](../executions/README.md) hold separate results and verification records. [directive.md](directive.md) links them, the runnable test project, the defect report and the release checklist. The [optional media archive](../supporting-media/README.md) labels older screenshots and recordings with their original run records. Extra media is not required for the default verification to finish.
